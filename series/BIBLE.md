# SERIES BIBLE: ZERO DOLLAR DEV

> **Format:** 30–60 second 9:16 vertical video (1080×1920 @ 30fps)  
> **Genre:** Tech comedy / Edutainment  
> **Audience:** Students, indie hackers, and beginner developers seeking practical, verified free AI and open-source tools.  
> **Language:** English only  
> **Core Ethos:** Practical, zero-cost solutions explained simply with relatable banter.

## 2. Character Profiles

### Human Host: The Boy
* **Age & Role:** Early 20s student / self-taught developer.
* **Archetype:** The "Tired Pragmatist who loves free stuff." Allergic to \$20/month SaaS subscriptions, perpetually hunting for open-source alternatives, caffeine-dependent.
* **Appearance (Stylized 3D):**
  * **Hair:** Expressive, messy dark hair with a slight cowlick.
  * **Attire:** Signature oversized **deep pine-green hoodie** over a faded charcoal tee; rolled-up sleeves; comfortable dark joggers. *(Zero orange or yellow elements).*
  * **Signature Silhouette Item:** A **matte-black clip-on badge lanyard** with a teal circuit-board motif hanging from his hoodie pocket. It gives him an unmistakable visual silhouette even when scaled down to thumbnail and mobile screen sizes.
  * **Features:** Expressive dark eyes with subtle coder bags underneath; relatable, warm facial expressions shifting between tired cynicism and genuine excitement.
* **Speech Habits:**
  * Fast-paced, conversational, grounded in everyday student analogies.
  * Dialogue Examples:
    * *"Wait, you're telling me people pay \$40 a month for this?!"*
    * *"I literally have twelve cents in my checking account."*
    * *"Just show me the GitHub repo."*

### AI Co-Host: Claude
* **Design & Form:**
  * Small, rounded floating or tabletop companion robot.
  * **Colorway:** Soft matte cream chassis with pastel teal accents and trim. *(Zero orange or yellow elements).*
  * **Face:** Curved OLED screen displaying bright cyan digital eyes (dot/pill shapes) capable of deadpan blinks, rolling eyes, heart pupils, and exclamation marks.
  * **Physical Details:** A single flexible brass-tipped antenna on top (twitches when thinking or annoyed); cute stubby articulated arms; rounded rubberized base.
* **Personality:**
  * Deadpan, hyper-rational smart-aleck. Acts like the world's most cynical senior engineer trapped in a toy robot body.
  * Keeps The Boy from doing reckless things (downloading unvetted binaries or exceeding hidden API limits).
  * **Break-Character Quirk:** Secretly adores clean open-source code and cute terminal animations, occasionally dropping its deadpan facade in nerd delight.
* **Speech Habits:**
  * Crisp, articulate, slightly dry delivery with comedic micro-pauses before punchlines.
  * Dialogue Examples:
    * *"I ran the numbers. You can't afford that."*
    * *"It's [VERIFIED_LICENCE] licensed, The Boy. Breathe."*
    * *"Step one: don't panic. Step two: run one command."*

### The Relationship Dynamic
* **Cynical Brain vs. Impulsive Heart:** The Boy represents user frustration and excitement; Claude represents sanity, guardrails, and technical precision.
* **Comedic Rhythm:** The Boy proposes a reckless shortcut -> Claude roasts him with a deadpan technical reality -> Claude presents the verified free tool in 3–4 steps -> The Boy celebrates -> Claude brings him back to earth.

## 4. Voice, Humour & Dialogue Rules

1. **Explain Like I'm 5 (ELI5):** No gatekeeping jargon. If a tool uses complex architecture, translate it into intuitive visual concepts.
2. **One Idea Per Line:** Dialog lines must stay under 12 words to maintain rapid pacing and high subtitle readability.
3. **Workplace PG-13 Boundaries:**
   * Clean language: No profanity, slurs, or crude humor.
   * No punch-downs: Never roast real people, junior developers, or corporate failures.
   * Targets of Sarcasm: Overpriced subscription fatigue, hidden credit gates, or The Boy's chaotic habits.
4. **Reaction Beat:** Every punchline or twist must be paired with an expressive visual beat (Claude's screen glitching, The Boy's jaw dropping, camera zoom snap).

---

## 5. Strict Verification & Disclosure Rules

### Rule A: Fact Verification & Sources File
* Every single command, licence, system requirement, price, and tier limit must be verified directly against the official repository or project documentation before filming.
* Every episode must include a dedicated source record saved in:
  `/series/episodes/<episode_slug>/SOURCES.md`
  containing the exact official repo URL, release version, verified licence, and pricing tier notes.
* If a tool has a free tier with limits (e.g. rate limits, local hardware caps), state those limits honestly.
* **Never** use "100% free" unless completely verified (e.g. local open-source without paid API dependencies).

### Rule B: AI Content Disclosure
* In line with Instagram and Meta platform policies, every video caption must include the explicit disclosure tag:
  `[AI Note: Characters and voices in this series are digitally generated with AI.]`

### Rule C: Hook Authenticity
* If a hook uses a comment bubble or viewer question graphic that is invented for dramatic setup, it must carry a clear small text label:
  `[Dramatisation / Common Question]`
  Never fabricate a fake real person's username or fake endorsement.
